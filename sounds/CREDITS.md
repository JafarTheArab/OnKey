# Sound credits

The piano and voice sounds in this folder (`piano/`, `oohs/`, `aahs/`) are single notes taken from the
**Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont** by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered to MP3 by Benjamin Gleitzman in
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (instruments: Acoustic Grand Piano,
Voice Oohs, Choir Aahs). Only every third note is included; OnKey plays the notes in between by speeding
up or slowing down the nearest one.

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/) (as stated by
midi-js-soundfonts; the original Fluid R3 SoundFont is under the MIT license). No changes were made to the
recordings other than selecting notes.
